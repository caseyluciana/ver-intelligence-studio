Shadow FTE Index

Every vendor relationship requires internal bandwidth to manage — meetings, reviews, escalations, oversight. This index makes that hidden cost visible. The true cost of a vendor engagement is the invoice plus the internal FTE hours it consumes.

Vendor spend: Coupa DEV · FY27 actuals (point-in-time snapshot)  |  Management hours: VER analyst estimates
What leadership needs to know right now
👻
What is Shadow Cost?
The annual value of internal employee time spent managing a vendor. Calculated as: (management hours/week × 52 / 2080) × blended annual rate for that employee level. This cost is real but never appears on an invoice.
🔥
The Friction Index
Shadow cost as a percentage of the vendor invoice. A Friction Index above 20% means managing the vendor costs more than 20 cents for every dollar spent. Above 30% is a governance and ROI problem, not a vendor management problem.
⚡
Why This Matters
Finance sees the vendor invoice. No system captures the director-level hours in weekly status calls, escalation bridges, and contract reviews. Surfacing this makes the true cost of complexity visible to the people who approve vendor spend.
