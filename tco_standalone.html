TCO Advisory
Contract value alone rarely reflects true cost. Hidden costs are real, measurable, and consistently underestimated at budget time. Gartner finds that implementation, integration, and support overhead typically add 40–75% above stated SaaS contract value. For SOW and professional services, IACCM benchmarks show total engagement cost runs 25–60% above the signed contract. For contingent workforce, SIA (Staffing Industry Analysts) reports that markup, compliance, and management overhead add 30–50% above the bill rate. For AI/agentic tools, a16z and Gartner data show engineering, governance, and over-licensing waste add 60–120% to raw API spend. Use this calculator before every renewal, new engagement, or strategic decision to surface the full picture.
