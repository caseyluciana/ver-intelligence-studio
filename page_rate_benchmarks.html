✨ AI Impact Beta
Off
Roles with significant AI/agentic tooling overlap are evolving rapidly — both in scope and market rate. Consider these factors when benchmarking AI-adjacent roles.
✨ Prompt Engineer / AI Ops
Market rates for these roles are increasing 15–25% annually and benchmarks have a shorter shelf life than traditional tech roles. Use the most recent data point available.
✨ ML Platform / MLOps Engineer
Premium over traditional platform engineering runs 18–30% in the current market. Verify whether the rate includes model infrastructure experience or just ops tooling.
✨ Agentic Workflow Developer
Emerging role category — limited benchmark data. Proxy using Senior Software Engineer + 10–20% premium. Validate with 3 competitive quotes before finalizing.
✨ AI-Augmented Traditional Roles
If a vendor claims AI augmentation justifies a higher rate for a standard role, request evidence of output productivity gain. Augmentation should lower cost-per-output, not raise the bill rate.
