D04 DA&O AI Operating Model
AI Strategy & Ops DA&O · Portable to DET Strategic Deliverable Spec
How DA&O runs AI.
And how any org can too.
DA&O's framework for AI decision-making, governance, and accountability — built in-house, proven in practice, designed so any team in DET can adopt the same playbook.
📍 The problem it solves

Right now, even within DA&O, AI adoption runs as a collection of parallel efforts — each initiative making its own build vs. buy decisions, using different tools, measuring success differently, and carrying risk that no one has a full view of. There's no shared language for what "AI-ready" looks like, no standard for how we evaluate a new tool, and no single place to see what's running, what it's costing, and what it's returning.

The DA&O AI Operating Model creates that shared foundation — starting here. It's not a bureaucratic review layer — it's the infrastructure that makes AI adoption in DA&O consistent, measurable, and defensible. And once it works here, any team in DET can lift and apply the same framework.

🏗️ What the model covers

Five components, each answering a specific governance question:

01
AI Readiness TiersHow mature is each team's AI capability right now? Tier 1 (exploratory) through Tier 4 (production-scale, measured). Tiers determine what support, review, and autonomy a team gets.
02
Build / Buy / Configure Decision FrameworkA repeatable model for the "which tool" conversation — total cost, build risk, dependency risk, customization ceiling. Prevents both over-building and over-subscribing.
03
Governance LayerApproval thresholds by spend and risk tier. Security and privacy review gates. New vendor eval checkpoints that route through AI Strategy & Ops before a license hits procurement.
04
Accountability StructureFor every active AI use case: a named owner, a spend envelope, a quality bar, a risk classification. One row in the registry (D09) per use case — one person accountable per row.
05
Value Measurement StandardStandardized ROI methodology that ties every use case to at least one of: time saved, cost avoided, quality improved. Quarterly rollup feeds the executive readout. Powered by the ROI & Value Scorecard (D02).

🔗 How it connects to the other deliverables

The operating model is the governing layer. It doesn't operate standalone — it reads from D01 (what use cases are active), D02 (what value are they generating), D09 (what vendors and licenses are in play), and surfaces that combined picture as the DA&O AI Health Score.

Think of D04 as the policy layer that makes all the other deliverables add up to something Roger can act on — not just a collection of outputs, but a managed portfolio. And because it's modular by design, any DET org that wants to run the same playbook can plug in their own data.
