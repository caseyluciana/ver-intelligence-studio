Why a Market Comparison Undersells It — Most of This Doesn't Exist Off-the-Shelf
Enterprise vendor intelligence platforms (Spend HQ, Ariba, Jaggaer, Beeline) solve for procurement workflows. They don't cover CW rate benchmarking at role-level granularity, exit optionality scoring, cinematic QBR generation, operating yield analysis, or the DET third-party spend context baked into every module. The $930K–$2.18M market estimate assumes a custom build — it can't be bought. And even then, a custom build would take 18–24 months with an implementation partner and a procurement cycle just to get started.
Tailored, Not Generic
Every module is purpose-built for DET third-party spend — not a generic procurement SaaS configured to fit. Rate benchmarks reflect real AMER CW roles. Risk scoring maps to actual DET vendor dependencies. No off-the-shelf tool ships with this context baked in.
Scope That Doesn't Exist in One Product
No single platform covers: vendor intelligence hub + stakeholder portal + cinematic QBR generator + exit optionality scoring + operating yield analysis + team CoS dashboard. Approximating this scope would require 4–6 separate tools, multiple integrations, and significant custom development — at multiple contract cycles and multiple vendors.
Continuity and Speed No Vendor Offers
First module live in under a day. New capability ships in hours, not quarters. There is no implementation partner meeting, no change request, no sprint planning. The gap between "we need this" and "this is live" collapsed to near-zero — a structural advantage no SaaS vendor can replicate.
