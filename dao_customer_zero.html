DA&O doesn't sit in the Customer Zero spotlight — it builds and maintains the foundation that makes Customer Zero execution possible. Vendor relationships managed, programs coordinated, portfolios prioritized, M&A integrations absorbed without disruption, ecosystems kept operational. Every function in Roger's org exists to reduce friction on the inside so the rest of DET can face outward without obstruction.

Our involvement in the Customer Zero strategy is internal by design. When a DET pillar executes Customer Zero effectively — when they can move fast, stay aligned, and deliver without noise — DA&O is part of what made that possible. The work doesn't always have a stage. It has a foundation.

"We keep the wheel turning on the inside so they can push things outside."
