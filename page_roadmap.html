🎬 Change Management Approach
How VER brings stakeholders on the journey
🎯
AI-Assisted Stakeholder Surveys
AI-assisted surveys deployed at scale to gather stakeholder friction points across DET. Structured data collection replaces manual interviews — surfacing patterns and engagement gaps that would otherwise take months to identify.
🎬
Cinematic QBRs
Quarterly business reviews reimagined — replacing hour-long meetings and slide decks with on-demand, narrative-driven intelligence experiences. QBR insights delivered through the Intelligence Hub, consumed on leadership's own time.
🧠
Self-Service Agents
AI-powered self-service for managers — answering engagement questions, surfacing contract terms, and guiding pre-contract evaluation without requiring direct VER intervention for every request.
🚀
VER Vision Series
An on-demand roadshow bringing VER's strategy and vision directly to DET leadership and stakeholders. Episodic, accessible, and built for consumption on the viewer's schedule — not a calendar invite.
