What VER Governs Analyst-curated
VER provides formal governance only where VER holds advisory scope — meaning a live SOW, MSP engagement, or direct contract relationship exists and VER can generate and act on real performance data.

Current formal governance scope:
NTT Data — AD restructure (24 POs) and AMS 5yr contract

What VER Does Not Govern Analyst-curated
VER monitors but does not formally govern the broader DET vendor portfolio from SOW, MSP, and Fieldglass data. This provides visibility into PO expiry, spend actuals, engagement status, and risk signals — without formal governance commitments where VER does not hold advisory scope.

Vendors outside governance scope receive:
Portfolio-level monitoring via Risk Intelligence
Renewal horizon tracking and spend alerts
No SLA review or remediation obligation

✅ What VER Commits To
1
Quarterly Intelligence Release — no meeting required
VER publishes a full engagement intelligence release on the first working day of each quarter. Pillar Leads and leadership receive it directly. The data speaks — no QBR scheduling friction.
2
SLA monitoring on every SOW-visible engagement
Every engagement within VER governance scope has defined SLA metrics tracked quarterly. When performance deviates, VER flags it, documents it, and tracks remediation.
3
Renewal intelligence 90+ days in advance
VER provides renewal analysis and recommendation at least 90 days before contract expiry for all governed engagements — giving Pillar Leads and leadership time to act, not react.
4
Transparent scope — clear about what we do and don't own
VER does not overstate its role. Where VER monitors but does not govern, we say so explicitly. Pillar Leads should always know whether VER is their accountability partner or an observing intelligence layer.
