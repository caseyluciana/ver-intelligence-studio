► Scoring Methodology & Legend
Risk Bands
Healthy (0–35): Low risk — standard VER monitoring cadence.
Monitor (36–55): Elevated risk — VER advisory engagement recommended within 30 days.
High Risk (56+): Action required — VER escalation recommended. Pillar Lead should be engaged.
Five Dimensions
🚚 Delivery: SLA performance, milestone delivery, attrition, escalations, evidence quality.
💰 Financial: Spend concentration, rate vs benchmark, budget variance, invoice compliance.
🤝 Relationship: Responsiveness, key person dependency, transparency, requirements comprehension.
📊 Market: Vendor financial health, competitive alternatives, rate trend vs market.
📋 Compliance: SOW currency, security documentation, portal registration, CO governance.
Score Types
✓ VER Assessed: Score from a formal 20-question VER assessment. Most reliable signal.
◎ Derived: Computed from vendor data fields. Indicative only. Perform a formal assessment for a definitive score.
Renewal proximity: Vendors expiring within 180 days receive a compliance risk adjustment.
