01 — The Problem

DET is adopting AI. Nobody owns it.

The tools are here. The enthusiasm is real. But across nine technology pillars, AI adoption is happening without a shared accountability structure, a value framework, or a function that asks the hardest questions.

🎯
No use case governance
Teams are picking AI use cases opportunistically, not strategically. There's no function filtering signal from noise or validating that the right problems are being solved.
📊
No value accountability
Leadership is being asked to show AI ROI with no centralized tracking, no baseline metrics, and no common framework for measuring what "working" actually means.
🔄
Pillars stuck in process
Individual orgs are re-solving the same problems and navigating the same friction — without a shared operating model to learn from or build on.
🏗
Builds without owners
AI-powered tools get built, shipped, and quietly abandoned. No function owns ongoing quality, maintenance, documentation, or iteration.
💰
No spend visibility
Token costs, API consumption, and AI licensing spend are scattered across orgs with no centralized view. Nobody can answer "how much are we spending on AI and is it worth it?" with confidence.
👥
Vendor & tool sprawl
AI tools and licenses are being acquired pillar by pillar with no consolidated registry, no renewal governance, and no one asking whether the org is paying for the same capability twice.
💡
T&E does awareness and enablement — and does it well. AMAs, peer learning series, the enablement cohorts. That's the knowing layer. What's missing is the doing layer — the space between "we know AI exists" and "we can prove it's working."

04 — Build Philosophy

The methodology that earned the room.

Not "we use AI tools." A deliberate, orchestrated multi-model pipeline — each AI doing exactly what it does best. This is what AI Ops in practice looks like, and it's already proven in production.

Stage 01
Gemini / GPT
Ideate & Strategize
Wide-aperture ideation. Framework exploration, research synthesis, competitive context. Best for breadth, creative divergence, and early-stage "what should we even be thinking about."
Brainstorm Research Frameworks
Stage 02
Claude Code
Execute & Build
Precision execution. Agentic, production-quality builds with full context retention, deep iteration cycles, and the judgment to know when a solution is actually done. Best for shipping real things that hold.
Build Iterate Ship
Stage 03
Codex
QA & Quality Test
Validation and quality assurance. Test generation, regression analysis, code review, edge case coverage. Ensures what was built holds under real conditions before it touches anyone's workflow.
QA Test Validate
⚡
This methodology generated org president-level visibility — not because of the tools, but because of the intentionality behind how they're used. Each model has a specific job. The output is production-quality, documented, and reproducible. That's the difference between "using AI" and running AI Ops.
🎯
Why this matters for the team: AI Strategy & Ops doesn't just recommend this methodology — we model it, document it, and help other DET teams adopt it. We become the org's living proof of concept for what rigorous, high-output AI execution actually looks like.
05 — Outputs

Concrete deliverables. Not slide decks.

Every team must answer "what did you make?" Here's ours. Most of it already exists or is in active development — this proposal formalizes the mandate behind work that's already happening.

  • 01
    AI Use Case Library
    A living, curated repository of validated AI use cases across DET — what works, what doesn't, what's worth pursuing, and why. The org's single source of truth for "should we build this?"
  • 02
    ROI & Value Scorecard Framework Casey & Judette Platz
    Standardized methodology for measuring AI initiative impact — time saved, cost avoided, quality improved, decisions accelerated. Co-owned and co-maintained by Casey Luciana and Judette Platz. Directly answers the question leadership is asked every quarter.
  • 03
    Build Documentation Standard
    Every AI tool built under this team is documented — what it does, how it was built, who owns it, how it's maintained, and how it's measured. No more abandoned builds or tribal knowledge walking out the door.
  • 04
    DET AI Operating Model
    A repeatable playbook for how DET ideates, selects, builds, and validates AI initiatives. Reduces per-pillar friction, creates shared language, and makes every org faster at doing this well.
  • 05
    Executive Intelligence Layer
    AI-powered tools and dashboards that give leadership real-time visibility into team performance, budget, vendor intelligence, and workforce dynamics. The VER Intelligence Hub is example one.
  • 06
    AI Investment Decision Framework
    A clear, repeatable process for evaluating and approving AI investments — right size, right use, right timing. Prevents org money being spent on tools without a defensible value case attached.
  • 07
    AI Spend & Token Tracking Dashboard
    Real-time visibility into API consumption, cost-per-use-case, and model spend across DET. Budget envelopes by pillar with spend thresholds and reforecast cadence. Nobody is running blind on AI costs on our watch.
  • 08
    Budget Envelope Governance
    Defined AI budget allocations per pillar with guardrails, approval thresholds, and a reforecast rhythm tied to Roger's quarterly readout. Spend stays visible, accountable, and justified.
  • 09
    AI Vendor & Licensing Registry
    Centralized registry of every AI tool, vendor contract, and license in use across DET — renewal dates, cost, utilization, and compliance status. Consolidation opportunities surfaced proactively. No surprise renewals, no duplicate spend. This is where AI Ops meets VER's vendor intelligence capability.
