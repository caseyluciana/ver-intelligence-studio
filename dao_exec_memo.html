What I Am Proposing

A named function — DA&O Strategy & Operations — formalized within the DA&O org under four pillars. This function sits within DA&O, reports through the SVP chain, and serves the full DA&O and DET portfolio.

Pillar 01
VER Ops — Vendor intelligence, rate benchmarking, SOW review & estimation, preferred supplier program, contract renewal oversight across the full AMER portfolio.
Pillar 02
Financial Intelligence — $68M technology & investment portfolio management, TCO modeling, budget forecasting, cost savings tracking, cost avoidance, and demand planning tied to workforce policy.
Pillar 03
AI Ops — Platform development & governance, ROI measurement, AI spend oversight, build methodology and documentation. DA&O is Customer Zero.
Pillar 04
DA&O Strategy — Portfolio monitoring, strategic execution, executive reporting cadence, AI use case library, and the DET AI operating model that scales this beyond DA&O.
The Ask

I am asking for formal recognition of DA&O Strategy & Operations as a named function — with a charter that reflects the four-pillar scope, title alignment that matches the level of responsibility I am already carrying, and a clear reporting structure under your org. The platform is built, the processes are running, and the track record is documented. The only thing missing is the mandate. I am ready to walk through the full proposal, answer every question, and align on timing. The work is already here. I am asking for the structure to match it.
