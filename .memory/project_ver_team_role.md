The VER team manages functions **within** VER (vendor engagement & reliability) and exists to serve **stakeholders** — business leaders, PMs, finance partners at Salesforce DET. They are not the owners of every vendor action; they provide intelligence, governance support, FinOps analysis, and risk visibility to the people who are.

**Why:** This affects how metrics, request language, and the portal should be framed. "Intake requests" come FROM stakeholders, not vendors. Response time SLAs are about how fast VER responds to stakeholder asks. Savings are identified on behalf of stakeholders, not owned end-to-end by VER.

**How to apply:**
- Frame intake/response as "stakeholder requests" not generic "vendor requests"
- Avoid language implying VER owns vendor actions (approving, processing, submitting)
- Assignment check types and tracker copy should reflect the service model: VER does the analysis, the stakeholder acts on it
- Hero/sub copy on tracker pages should reference serving stakeholders
