VER-Ready Manager Guide
Everything a business owner or stakeholder needs to engage VER correctly — from first contact through sourcing certification. The pre-contract checklist is a mandatory governance gate.
For Business Owners & Stakeholders ▼ Governance Gate April 2026
What it means to be VER-Ready: VER is the first engagement point for all third-party initiatives. The Pre-Contract Cleanliness Checklist is a mandatory governance gate ensuring every engagement is right-sized, fiscally disciplined, and strategically aligned before sourcing is triggered.
Why It Matters
⚡
Velocity
Pre-vetting your request prevents downstream rework, budget surprises, and legal bottlenecks before they occur. Engaged early, VER accelerates the path to sourcing.
🎯
Integrity
Ensures your project is backed by a validated business case with a FinOps lens applied to cost impact. No engagement proceeds without a verified budget line and demand forecast.
🛡
Reliability
VER certifies that vendors you choose meet the highest standards of trust and business continuity. Every certified engagement carries a VER seal of governance compliance.
The 4-Step Engagement Process
1
Engagement
Contact VER at the Identify Need stage — before any sourcing activity begins.
2
Validation
Complete all 5 checklist modules with VER. Resolve any open items before proceeding.
3
Certification
Once all items are resolved, VER issues Certified status for the engagement.
4
Sourcing
Sourcing receives the certified request and focuses solely on contractual negotiation.
What VER Reviews: Every purchase request, SOW, MSP contract, or M&A transition is pre-vetted for DET Roadmap alignment and compliance. This allows Sourcing to focus 100% on fiduciary negotiation, knowing operational foundations are already defined.
✅ VER Pre-Contract Cleanliness Checklist
Five modules · All items required before Sourcing engagement
Required Before Sourcing ⬇ Download Checklist
Important: This checklist must be verified by VER before Sourcing engagement. If any item remains unchecked, the request cannot proceed to sourcing. Contact the VER team to initiate certification.
🗺
Module 1 — Strategic Portfolio & Roadmap Alignment
Focus: Ensuring we turn strategy into outcomes reliably and predictably
Portfolio ID / Project Code
Verified official ID from the Master Portfolio list
Roadmap Slotting
Confirmed work is scheduled for the current fiscal year/quarter
Redundancy Check
Confirmed this capability does not already exist within DET
Executive Sponsorship
DET Pillar Lead/VP has signed off on this roadmap addition
💰
Module 2 — FinOps, Budget & Financial Transparency
Focus: Driving financial discipline and cost optimization
Budget Line Item
Verified specific Budget Owner and Cost Center
Funding Status
Confirmed as "Budgeted" — unbudgeted requires Leadership approval
Demand Forecast
12-month projected consumption for spend tracking
Value Realization Plan
Documented plan for measuring "Savings vs. Performance" post-launch
Unit Economics
Defined baseline cost-per-unit for monthly variance reporting
⚖
Module 3 — Risk & Compliance Guardrails
Focus: Establishing trusted governance and risk controls
Co-Employment Shield
SOW explicitly states vendor manages their own staff and tools
Data Sensitivity Tier
Flagged if vendor touches PII/PCI — triggers InfoSec Audit
SLA/KPI Definition
Top 3–5 reliability metrics with clear service credit penalties
Right to Audit
Clause included for annual performance and financial audits
Supplier Risk Gate
High-risk vendors pre-vetted by VER Risk Intelligence before approval
🏢
Module 4 — M&A & High-Risk Situations
Focus: Ensuring a resilient, compliant, and observable technology environment
Legacy Sunset Date
Hard date for terminating legacy contracts to eliminate waste
Transition Service Agreement (TSA)
Clear hand-off dates for interim support during transitions
Business Continuity (BCP)
Verified vendor has a validated BCP for access loss or disruption
Escrow Requirements
Source code escrow required for mission-critical software
🔧
Module 5 — MSP, SOW & Project Specifics
Focus: Ensuring every initiative has a clear owner and measurable impact
Acceptance Criteria
Clear "Definition of Done" for every project milestone
Payment Triggers
Tied to measurable deliverables, not just time spent
Resource Requirements
Internal DET leads identified for implementation oversight
Knowledge Transfer
Documented plan for training internal staff before vendor exit
