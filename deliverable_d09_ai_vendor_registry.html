📍 The problem it solves

AI spend in most orgs is invisible. Tools get expensed individually, subscriptions auto-renew on credit cards, and no one has a consolidated view of what's running, what it's costing, or whether it ever went through a security review. By the time finance asks the question, it's already a problem.

The bigger risk is the tools no one is watching. A team that signed up for an AI platform two years ago, gave it access to sensitive vendor data, and never filed a review — that's not a theoretical gap. It's a live compliance exposure. The registry makes those entries visible before they become incidents.

The "no review filed" row in the registry below is intentional. That's what this deliverable exists to surface. Every executive who sees it knows immediately why a registry matters — and why it has to be maintained by a function with a mandate, not managed ad-hoc.

🏗️ What each entry contains

Eight fields per tool — everything needed for a spend, risk, and renewal decision. No detective work required.

Vendor / Tool
Full name, vendor, and team using it.
Category
LLM Platform · Code Intelligence · Productivity · Research & Data
License Type
Enterprise / Team / Individual / API usage-based
Est. Annual Cost
Total cost across all seats/instances — normalized to annual.
Renewal Date
Hard renewal date with 90/60/30-day alert flags.
Owner
Named person — one owner per entry, no "TBD."
Risk Tier
Low / Medium / High — based on data access, DLP controls, sensitivity.
Governance Status
Reviewed ✓ · Pending Review ⚠ · No Review Filed ✗
🔐 How tools enter the registry

New AI tools must route through the AI Strategy & Ops intake process (D04 Governance Layer) before they receive a registry entry — and before they receive budget approval. No review on file means no entry. No entry means no sanctioned use.

This is the mechanism that stops the "no review filed" rows from multiplying. Any tool already in use without a review gets a remediation window — 30 days to file a retroactive review or sunset the tool from DA&O workflows.

📊 What it produces
💰
Total AI Spend Visibility — Consolidated view of all AI licensing costs across DA&O. First time this number has existed in one place. Feeds directly into the executive readout and budget planning cycles.
📅
Renewal Calendar — Every contract renewal flagged 90, 60, and 30 days out. No more auto-renewal surprises. Proactive decisions replace reactive scrambles.
🛡️
Risk Exposure Dashboard — How many active tools have no review on file, what data they access, and what the remediation path is. The number that makes security and legal pay attention.
🔗 How it connects to the other deliverables

D09 is the source of record for vendor-level intelligence that feeds D04 (the Operating Model). The Build/Buy/Configure framework (D04 Component 02) reads from the registry before recommending a new tool — if we're already paying for something that covers the use case, that's the answer, not a new vendor. D01 (Use Case Library) links to registry entries so every use case has a named tool and cost on file.

Think of D09 as the financial and risk layer — it's what turns the operating model from a framework into a managed spend portfolio that Roger can bring to Joe with real numbers.
