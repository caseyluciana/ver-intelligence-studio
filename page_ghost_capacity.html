📜 Methodology — How Every Number Is Calculated ▼
Ghost Capacity %
Ghost % = (Expected Output Score − Actual Output Score) ÷ Expected Output Score × 100
Measures the relative performance gap against what was expected — not a raw inverse of the output score. A pillar with actual=71 and expected=75 has a 5.3% ghost rate, not 29%. This is the only defensible framing for leadership: it accounts for realistic expectations, not a theoretical 100% benchmark.
Gap Classification
Critical: Ghost % > 30%  |  Medium: 15–30%  |  Aligned: < 15%
Thresholds are calculated automatically from the Ghost % formula — never manually assigned. This eliminates classification inconsistencies caused by hardcoded status values in the data.
Capital at Risk
Capital at Risk = Sum of (CWF Spend + Vendor Spend) for all Critical Gap pillars
The total investment in pillars where spend materially exceeds attributable output. This is spend under review, not confirmed waste — it surfaces where leadership conversations should be happening. Not every dollar is unproductive; the number sets the scope of inquiry.
CWF Mix
CWF Mix = CWF Spend ÷ (CWF Spend + Vendor Spend) × 100
Shows sourcing composition — what share of the pillar's investment is contingent workforce versus direct vendor spend. High CWF mix in a critical gap pillar may indicate staff-aug model risk. This is a composition measure, not a spend efficiency measure.
Output Score
A 0–100 analyst-estimated composite of delivery quality, milestone completion rate, and SLA adherence for the pillar. There is no single authoritative system of record — scores are VER analyst assessments based on program status reporting and stakeholder feedback. Confidence is directional; treat scores as relative comparisons, not absolute measurements.
Throughput Delta
The percentage point change between actual output score and expected output score. A negative delta means the pillar is delivering below its performance contract. Displayed separately from Ghost % to preserve the raw gap signal alongside the normalized rate.
Data Transparency
Spend figures are VER analyst estimates aligned to Coupa pillar/category groupings. Output scores are directional estimates — not sourced from a program management system. This view is designed to surface conversations and prioritize review, not replace a formal program health dashboard. All pillar data is editable in the Manage Pillars drawer.
