▶ Scoring Methodology & Legend
Risk Bands
Healthy (0–35): Low risk — standard VER monitoring cadence. No immediate action required.
Monitor (36–55): Elevated risk — VER is actively watching. Advisory engagement recommended within 30 days.
High Risk (56+): Action required — VER escalation recommended. Pillar Lead should be engaged immediately.
Five Dimensions
🚚 Delivery: SLA performance, milestone delivery rate, attrition, escalation frequency, evidence quality.
💰 Financial: Spend concentration, rate vs benchmark, budget variance, invoice compliance.
🤝 Relationship: Responsiveness, key person dependency, transparency, requirements comprehension.
📊 Market: Vendor financial health, competitive alternatives, rate trend vs market.
📋 Compliance: SOW currency, security documentation, portal registration, change order governance.
Score Types
✓ VER Assessed: Score derived from a formal 20-question VER assessment. All five dimensions independently evaluated. Most reliable signal.
◎ Derived: Score computed automatically from vendors.csv fields — SLA score, tier, category, spend, renewal proximity, and existing risk fields. Indicative only. Perform a formal VER assessment for a definitive score.
Renewal proximity: Vendors expiring within 180 days automatically receive a compliance risk adjustment — +8 pts (90–180 days), +15 pts (<90 days), +25 pts (expired).
