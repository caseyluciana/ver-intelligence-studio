The Function

What VER Actually Does

The distinction isn’t about scope — it’s about accountability structure. Different axis entirely.

🏗️
PPMs Own

Roadmaps, timelines, delivery. Column-oriented — each PPM is accountable to one pillar, top to bottom.

🔎
VER Owns

Intelligence types — across every pillar simultaneously. Row-oriented — TCO, risk, FinOps, governance delivered horizontally.

⏱️
When PPMs Act

After approval. PPMs build plans and execute against committed roadmaps. Their accountability begins at approval.

⚡
When VER Acts

Before decisions lock. VER intelligence is upstream of commitment — shaping the decision before the plan is built.
