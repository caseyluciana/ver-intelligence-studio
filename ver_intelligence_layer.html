The Function

What Cross-Pillar Intelligence Actually Does

Not about scope — about accountability structure. Different axis entirely.

🏗️
PPMs Own

Roadmaps, timelines, delivery. Column-oriented — each PPM is accountable to one pillar, top to bottom.

🔎
This Layer Owns

Intelligence types across every pillar simultaneously. Row-oriented — TCO, risk, FinOps, governance delivered horizontally.

⏱️
When PPMs Act

After approval. PPMs build plans and execute against committed roadmaps. Their accountability begins at approval.

⚡
When This Layer Acts

Before decisions lock. Intelligence is upstream of commitment — shaping the decision before the plan is built.

FY28 Intelligence Roadmap

What Cross-Pillar Intelligence Builds Next Fiscal

The tooling infrastructure is operational. FY28 is about making it easy to reach — so any pillar can pull on it when they need it.

Renewal Horizon

Multi-year contract modeling available before renewal decisions lock. The signal is there when any pillar needs it — not after it’s too late to act.

TCO Intelligence

Total cost of ownership analysis available before engagements are scoped. The tool is live — ready when any pillar wants to reach for it.

SOW Intelligence

Role-level cost analysis available before engagements are planned. Upstream by design — there for any pillar that wants it early.

Governance Support

Regular vendor relationship review available on cadence or on request. Risk visible early — so it doesn’t have to surface reactively.

Stakeholder Intake

A standing intelligence resource, not an ad hoc one. Any pillar can bring a question — we’re here to answer it.

Cross-Pillar FinOps

Spend pattern visibility no single pillar has on its own. Available to the full portfolio — overlap and opportunity surfaced when it’s useful.
