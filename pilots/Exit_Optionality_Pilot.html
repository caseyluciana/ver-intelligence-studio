Concentration risk is the primary exit blocker
Vendors with embedded knowledge and no viable alternatives require 12–18 months of preparation before a commercial exit is viable. Identify these relationships before a business event forces the decision.

Transition cost above 30% signals structural lock-in
Transition cost as a % of annual spend is the most commonly underestimated exit factor. Costs above 30% signal that the relationship has lock-in beyond contract terms that must be addressed proactively.

Exit optionality scoring is a planning tool, not a termination signal. High scores show where preparation investment is needed most — not which vendors to exit.
