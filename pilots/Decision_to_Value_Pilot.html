What leadership needs to know about value realization
1
Execution is where value disappears
Value realization rarely fails at the decision stage — it fails at execution. The gap between "Approved" and "Realized" is where most savings evaporate. Every approved decision needs an owner, a target date, and a validation method before the approval meeting ends.
2
Cost avoidance is the most overclaimed category
Cost avoidance is the most commonly overclaimed value type. Without a validated baseline spend and a documented alternative, cost avoidance entries should remain in "Identified" until evidence is attached.
3
Realization rate is the most honest KPI
The realization rate KPI is the most honest signal of procurement effectiveness. A portfolio with high expected value and low realization rate signals a decision-making culture that approves without executing.
