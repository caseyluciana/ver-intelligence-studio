Strategic Intelligence
1
Utilization is the most controllable yield factor — and the most ignored at renewal
Vendors renewing at 78% utilization are effectively billing for capacity the business is not consuming. Validate utilization before every renewal negotiation — unused capacity is negotiation leverage.
2
Internal overhead is frequently excluded from yield calculations
A $4M vendor engagement with $800K of internal coordination cost has a true economic cost of $4.8M — a 20% uplift that changes the negotiation baseline. Always include management overhead in full economic cost.
3
The yield score is explainable by design
Every component is visible so stakeholders can challenge assumptions rather than accept a single number they cannot interrogate. The weakest component always points to the highest-leverage improvement opportunity.
