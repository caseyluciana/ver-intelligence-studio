What is Operating Yield? Operating Yield answers: what are we actually getting for the full economic cost of this engagement? Each assessment scores five weighted factors — utilization, output quality, reliability/SLA, friction (inverse), and risk exposure — to surface which vendor relationships deliver full value and which need renegotiation or exit.
Yield Assessment Tool
Yield Assumptions
Fields auto-populate from VER vendor data
Yield Factors (0 – 100)
Formula: (Utilization × 0.20) + (Output Quality × 0.35) + (Reliability × 0.15) + (100 − Friction × 0.15) + (100 − Risk × 0.15)
Inverse factors: Friction and Risk score high = bad; the formula subtracts them from 100 before weighting.
Utilization Rate
% of contracted capacity actually consumed
Output Quality
Outcome attainment & deliverable acceptance rate
Reliability / SLA
SLA compliance rate; from vendor sla_score
Friction Rate inverse
Management burden & escalation cost (high = bad)
Risk Exposure inverse
Financial + compliance risk composite (high = bad)
