01
01 — The Problem

DET is adopting AI.
Nobody owns it.

Across nine technology pillars, AI adoption is happening without a shared accountability structure, a value framework, or a function that asks the hardest questions.

🎯
No use case governance
Teams are picking AI use cases opportunistically, not strategically.
📊
No value accountability
No centralized tracking, no baseline metrics, no common framework for measuring what "working" means.
🔄
Pillars stuck in process
Individual orgs re-solving the same problems without a shared operating model to learn from.
🏗
Builds without owners
AI-powered tools get built, shipped, and quietly abandoned. No ongoing quality, maintenance, or iteration.
💰
No spend visibility
Token costs and AI licensing spend scattered across orgs. Nobody can answer "is it worth it?" with confidence.
👥
Vendor & tool sprawl
AI tools acquired pillar by pillar — no consolidated registry, no renewal governance, no consolidation view.
💡
T&E does awareness and enablement — and does it well. What's missing is the doing layer — the space between "we know AI exists" and "we can prove it's working."

    Executive Intelligence Layer
    AI-powered tools giving leadership real-time visibility into performance, budget, and vendor intelligence.

    AI Investment Decision Framework
    Clear process for evaluating AI investments — right size, right use, right timing. No spend without a value case.

    AI Spend & Token Tracking Dashboard
    Real-time API consumption, cost-per-use-case, and model spend across DET. Nobody runs blind on AI costs.

    Budget Envelope Governance
    Defined AI budget allocations per pillar with guardrails and reforecast rhythm tied to Roger's quarterly readout.
  • 09
    AI Vendor & Licensing Registry
    Centralized registry of every AI tool, vendor contract, and license — renewal dates, cost, utilization. No surprise renewals, no duplicate spend.
