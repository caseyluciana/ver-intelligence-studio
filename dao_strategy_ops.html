The Operating Model

Built on a model
already trusted.

DA&O Strategy & Ops runs like a Rapid Innovation Squad — small team, real Business Leader, named accountability, and a mandate that cuts across reporting lines.

👥
Real Business Leader. Not a Proxy.
The RIS non-negotiable: a Business Leader with deep conviction and dedicated weekly capacity. This function has one — with full ownership, not an added hat.
⚡
Small & Flat
2–3 active builders plus a Business Leader who makes decisions. No councils, no hand-off queues. Add layers, lose speed — so we don't.
🔗
Cuts Across Reporting Lines
Like any effective squad, this function serves DA&O and DET regardless of where the work lands on an org chart. The squad is one team, wherever its members report.
🎯
Focus & Accountability Built In
Quarterly cadence, ROI scorecard, named deliverables per pillar. Accountability isn't added on top — it's the operating constraint from day one.
