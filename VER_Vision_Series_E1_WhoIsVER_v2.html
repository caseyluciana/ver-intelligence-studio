The Name
Every letter is intentional.
The name reflects a standard, not just a function.
V
Vendor
E
Engagement
R
Reliability
Not vendor management. Not procurement. Not a vendor database. VER focuses on the engagements — the third-party work being done inside DET, the spend attached to it, and the reliability of the vendors delivering it. The emphasis on Reliability isn't semantic. It's the standard VER holds every engagement to.
The Definition
VER exists because
third-party spend is one of DET's largest
and most consequential cost levers.
DET engages third parties across every pillar — for project work, application maintenance, staff augmentation, consulting, and technology services. That spend is significant, it's active, and it requires a function that monitors engagements, advises on decisions, and holds vendors accountable to the standard DET deserves. VER is that function.
VER doesn't govern the portfolio. VER advises and monitors the engagements and spend within it — so that every dollar DET spends with a third party is visible, defensible, and working as hard as it should.
How VER Works
Three mandates. One function.
01
Engagement Intelligence
VER monitors active engagements and third-party spend across all 9 DET pillars ��� performance trends, spend patterns, reliability signals, and emerging risks. We surface what matters before it becomes a problem.
02
Strategic Advisory
When a Pillar Lead is considering bringing in a third party — for any reason, at any stage — VER should be in that conversation. We bring the intelligence, the market context, and the advisory perspective. The decision stays with the Pillar Lead.
03
Reliability Assurance
VER holds vendors accountable to the standard DET deserves — through Cinematic QBRs, performance monitoring, and spend analysis. When a vendor isn't delivering, VER says so. When they are, VER validates it.
When VER Engages
Not just at renewal. At the beginning.
VER's advisory value is highest before a commitment is made — not after. Here's when VER needs to be in the conversation.
Early Stage — Before Commitment
Any time a third party is being considered
This is where VER adds the most value. Before a SOW is written, before a vendor is selected, before scope is defined — VER should be advising.
New SOW or project engagement
Staff augmentation or resource requests
Consulting or advisory engagements
Any planned third-party spend
Active Engagement — During Delivery
While the work is being done
VER monitors active engagements continuously — not just at review time. Performance signals, spend patterns, and reliability indicators are tracked in real time.
SLA performance and delivery tracking
Attrition and continuity monitoring
Spend vs. MFT variance analysis
Change orders and scope shifts
Renewal & Transition — At Decision Points
When decisions need intelligence
At renewal, VER provides the full advisory picture — performance history, rate benchmarks, market alternatives, and a recommendation. This is when everything VER has been monitoring converges.
Renewal strategy and rate benchmarking
Vendor transition or replacement
Scope expansion or renegotiation
