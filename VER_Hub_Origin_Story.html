The Problem

We were managing $295M of third-party spend without a clear picture of any of it.

Every quarter, I spent hours hunting across spreadsheets, shared drives, email threads, and disconnected reports just to answer questions that should have instant answers: What does our renewal calendar look like? Which contracts are at risk? Where is our third-party spend actually going? How are our key vendors tracking against SLA? What does the next 12 months look like for the portfolio?

The answers existed — but they were scattered, stale, and completely invisible to the people who needed to act on them. There was no single source of truth. No live view. No platform that connected vendor performance to spend to risk to strategy in one place.

"I had to look in a thousand places to find information that should live in one place. The intelligence was never available, never surfaced, never actionable in real time. So I built it — not because I was asked to, but because it needed to exist."

Senior leaders were making decisions about multi-million-dollar engagements without the context they deserved. Stakeholders were submitting vendor requests with no visibility into cost, risk, or alternatives. Contracts were being renewed by default because no one had bandwidth to surface the right signals in time.

That is the gap the VER Intelligence Hub was built to close. Not a spreadsheet update. Not a better deck. A platform — designed to make third-party spend intelligence visible, accessible, and actionable at every level of the organization.

What Was Built

A full-stack intelligence platform. No engineering. No budget. No waiting.

The VER Intelligence Hub is not a dashboard. It is a portfolio command center — a suite of interconnected tools that give leadership, stakeholders, and the VER team a live, actionable view of every dimension of DET's third-party spend. Built in pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, it deploys as a single shareable link and runs entirely in the browser. Every page is printable. Every view is interactive.

And every module was designed to answer a real question that previously had no good answer.

Platforms like this — interactive, multi-module, executive-grade, data-driven, printable, stakeholder-facing — typically take months of engineering time, a dedicated BI team, and a formal product roadmap to produce. The VER Intelligence Hub was designed, built, iterated, and delivered by one person. No budget. No engineering dependency. No months of lead time.

What makes it significant isn't just the speed. It's that it's already in use. It's already answering questions that previously had no answer. It's already surfacing third-party spend intelligence that previously sat buried in spreadsheets. It's already changing how this organization talks about vendor strategy, renewal risk, and financial exposure.

And the platform is built to grow. Every module is designed to be extended. Every page is built to accept new data. The foundation is solid — the roadmap is clear — and the impact is already real.
