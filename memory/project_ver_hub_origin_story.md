Casey built the VER Intelligence Hub herself, first person. It is her build and her story, though the team uses it.

**The pitch moment:** Presenting to Joe (Org President) who is already excited ("stoked"). Goal is to maintain/grow that excitement and push for formal adoption across orgs. Senior leaders have already said they want it packaged and sold.

**Core narrative:** The information existed. The need existed. The stakeholders existed. The connective tissue never did — Casey built it.

**Who it's for:**
- Casey's team (VER) — what they do for stakeholders every day
- Stakeholders who couldn't see vendor/spend/risk intelligence with this level of transparency
- Tools at this scale simply didn't exist before

**Why she built it:**
- Had to look in a thousand places to find information that should live in one place
- The intelligence was never available, never surfaced, never actionable in real time
- Built it because it needed to exist — not because she was asked to

**Desired outcomes from Joe office hours (confirmed Aug 2026):**
- Casey's goal: show Joe what she built, *why* she built it, and how it makes DET better spend stewards — personal story, not a product pitch
- Alex/Seth/Nuno's goal: they see a sellable product and want Joe to see that too
- These are not in conflict — Casey's authentic story IS the pitch; don't separate them
- Timeline TBD — Casey is coordinating with Alex Labeck (Joe's Chief of Staff)

**Framing for the session:**
- Lead with the gap that existed, not the feature list
- The "why I built it" narrative carries more weight with Joe than any capability demo
- Spend stewardship is the through-line: VER's job is to be the org's intelligence layer on vendor spend — this hub makes that visible and actionable for the first time
- Packaging/commercial potential is real but secondary to Casey's personal story in this session

**Why:** Casey explicitly wants Joe to feel the story, not be sold to. When drafting anything for this presentation, anchor on the human problem first, capability second.
