The ROI slides (`VER_Hub_ROI_Slide.html` and `VER_Hub_ROI_Expanded.html`) tell the story of the VER Intelligence Hub's internal value at Salesforce.

**Audience:** Internal Salesforce stakeholders and leadership, including Joe (Org President) who is already excited about the platform.

**Goal:** Formal adoption of the hub as an org-sanctioned platform, and potential packaging for broader Salesforce orgs/BUs who manage vendor portfolios.

**Why:** Casey built this as a grassroots initiative. The ROI case supports elevating it from a personal build to an officially invested platform.

**Tone:** Do NOT pitch or sell. Every leader who has seen it already wants to invest, market, and sell it. The slides just need to show honest ROI — what it cost to build, what an equivalent platform would cost to buy, and what was delivered in that gap. Let the numbers speak.

**Key framing:** Built collaboratively by Casey + Claude (AI-assisted build). That's part of the story — the force multiplier angle. Enterprise vendor intelligence platforms cost $200K–$500K+. This was built in months at a fraction of that cost.

**How to apply:** ROI sections should be factual and grounded — before/after contrast, build cost vs. market equivalent, scope delivered. No "case for investment" language needed; the work makes its own case.
