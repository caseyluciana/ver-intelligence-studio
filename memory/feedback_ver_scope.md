VER does not process Change Orders (COs) or manage renewals. Never include CO Review, CO Generator, CO metrics, Renewal Horizon, or renewal processing language in team portal pages.

**Why:** VER tracks and maintains data on SOWs, COs, and renewals for FinOps and support purposes — but they do not submit, process, or own those actions. The team serves stakeholders who own those decisions.

**How to apply:** Remove CO Review, CO Generator, CO metrics, Renewal Horizon nav items, and "renewals actioned" output metrics from all team portal pages. On the main hub pages (index.html, page_renewal.html) this may still apply — the team portal is the main concern.
