Downstream Procurement Timelines
Not a VER process — the following steps are owned by Procurement and run after VER clears. Plan accordingly.
📋 Agreement Management, F&S BT, Sourcing, FT Order Admin & Order Admin — each team carries a minimum 5-business-day SLA for non-complex, standard-risk, standard-dollar engagements. 5 days ea.
⚠️ Complex, high-risk, or high-dollar engagements — each of those steps can take significantly longer. No fixed SLA applies. Varies
🔒 Security review — required for most software purchases. Timeline is separate and not included in Procurement's 30-day estimate. Varies
💰 Budget approvals & leadership sign-off — these are not included in Procurement's SLA estimates and run on their own timelines outside the standard process. Not included
💡 Procurement recommends starting the full approval cycle at least 30 days before your target start date — and that clock starts after VER clears. If you haven't submitted yet, submit to VER at least 45 days out.
